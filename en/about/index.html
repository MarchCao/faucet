About HAICE Faucet

HAICE Faucet supports overseas customers with faucet OEM / ODM manufacturing in China.

We work with faucet factories in China, coordinating product specifications, sampling, mass production, quality confirmation and lead-time management according to customer requirements.

Our partner factories have a production cooperation relationship with TOTO and maintain high standards of production management and quality requirements.

We provide Chinese, Japanese, English and Korean communication support to help overseas customers work smoothly with faucet manufacturing in China.

HAICE Faucet itself is not a factory, but a production support partner connecting you with partner factories in China. Actual manufacturing is done by the partner factories; we handle communication and end-to-end production coordination.
