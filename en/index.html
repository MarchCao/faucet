        How We Work

        We are not a factory. We stand between overseas customers and our partner faucet factories in China, handling communication and end-to-end production coordination.

        You (the customer)

        Overseas customers — faucet brands, trading companies, wholesalers, e-commerce sellers — bring us your requirements.

        HAICE Faucet

        Supporting requirement confirmation, quotation coordination, sample arrangement, production follow-up, quality communication and lead-time management in your language.

        Partner Faucet Factories in China

        Actual manufacturing, quality execution and shipment are handled by our partner factories in China. Our partner factories have a production cooperation relationship with TOTO and maintain high standards of quality management.
