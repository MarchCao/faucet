Privacy Policy

How HAICE Faucet handles personal information.

Information Collected

Information you provide via the inquiry form or email — such as your name, company, email address, country/region and message — is used only to respond to your inquiry.

Use of Information

The information collected is used to respond to inquiries and to communicate with you about quotations, sampling, production and related matters.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where sharing with partner factories is necessary to arrange production.

Analytics

This website may use access analytics to understand usage; no personally identifiable information is collected for this purpose.

Contact

If you have questions about how personal information is handled, please contact sales@haice.top.
