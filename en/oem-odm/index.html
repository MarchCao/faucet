OEM / ODM Faucet Manufacturing

The right manufacturing partnership for your needs.

OEM

What is OEM?

You provide the designs, specifications, samples or technical requirements; we coordinate production accordingly, faithfully reproducing what you asked for.

ODM

What is ODM?

Based on your needs, we help coordinate everything from product development to production within existing capabilities. The exact scope depends on the product — we'll confirm the details with you in advance.

We never exaggerate our design or R&D capabilities. We only commit to what we can actually deliver, and we state it clearly before we start.

What We Handle

Standing between you and the factory, we coordinate the entire flow so production runs smoothly.

Requirement & Specification Confirmation

We organize the product, structure, cartridge specifications, surface finishing, quantities, target pricing and lead times, and align them with the factory.

Quotation Coordination

We coordinate with the factory based on the confirmed details and provide you with a quotation.

Sample Arrangement

We arrange sampling and follow up so your revision requests are implemented quickly.

Mass Production Follow-up

We follow production scheduling, components, quality and lead times throughout.

Quality & Lead-Time Communication

We communicate quality confirmations and lead-time adjustments with the factory on your behalf.

Shipment Coordination

We help arrange shipment according to your order and destination.

Feel Free to Reach Out

Tell us your product category, estimated quantity or lead time — whatever you know. We'll reply in your language.
