Production Flow

The standard 8-step flow, from inquiry to shipment.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Tell us your product ideas and requirements via the form or email.

  2. 02

    Requirement Confirmation

    Confirm the product details.

    • Product
    • Structure
    • Cartridge specs
    • Surface finishing
    • Quantity
    • Target price
    • Lead time
  3. 03

    Quotation

    We coordinate with the factory based on the confirmed details and provide a quotation.

  4. 04

    Sampling

    Once the details are set, we arrange sample production.

  5. 05

    Sample Confirmation

    Please review the samples and share your revision requests.

  6. 06

    Mass Production

    After order confirmation, mass production begins.

  7. 07

    Quality Confirmation

    We carry out in-production quality confirmation and coordinate with the factory as needed.

  8. 08

    Shipment

    Shipment is arranged according to your order and destination.
